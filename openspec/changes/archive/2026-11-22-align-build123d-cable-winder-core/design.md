## Context

`build123d/simple/cable_winder.py` currently generates only the center spool. Its defaults match `openscad/cable_organizer/winder.scad`, but the build123d wire channels use simple centered tori rather than the offset, clipped `wireChannel()` geometry. The lower tapered flange is also reversed relative to `spoolSolid()` in OpenSCAD. The direct reference is `openscad/cable_organizer/winder_center.stl`, generated by `winder_center.scad`.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Bring the build123d center spool's channel profile and lower flange orientation close to the OpenSCAD reference.
- Preserve the existing parameter defaults, coordinate system, script entry point, and `build123d/simple/cable_winder.stl` output path.
- Keep a reproducible comparison against the OpenSCAD center-spool STL.

**Non-Goals:**
- Port the top and bottom housing halves, hooks, or assembled winder.
- Change the site's gallery or introduce dependencies.
- Require identical triangulation between OpenSCAD and build123d exports.

## Decisions

- Treat `winder_center.scad` and `winder_center.stl` as the reference, not the full assembled `winder.stl`.
- Replace the single-torus approximation with a build123d construction that follows the two translated/clipped revolved channel halves in `wireChannel()`; preserve the existing channel dimensions and vertical offset.
- Correct the lower taper so its large radius is at the bottom edge and it narrows toward the spool body, matching the OpenSCAD cone's `d1`/`d2` orientation.
- Verify the default model's validity, single-solid result, envelope, flange direction, and channel shape against the reference. Use section or mesh comparison to allow for tessellation differences.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- The OpenSCAD channel construction uses intersections and mirrored halves, so translating it into build123d may need careful Boolean ordering. Validate the result as one solid and inspect matching cross-sections.
- STL triangulation settings differ, so triangle counts and small mesh-level deviations are not parity criteria; prioritize profile landmarks, dimensions, and overall shape.
- **[Scope risk]** The center spool may still appear different when compared with the full winder assembly. Keep comparison focused on the center-only reference STL.
